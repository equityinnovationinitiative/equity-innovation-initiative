Why we exist

Our mission

To strengthen public confidence in government through equal accountability under the law, transparent oversight, and practical innovation that places the public interest above improper political or financial influence.

We believe strong institutions need consistent rules, trustworthy evidence, and the willingness to improve. The initiative is a proposed independent effort—not a government agency or political campaign.

Founding principles

Fairness is the standard. Innovation is the method.

01 / EQUAL ACCOUNTABILITY

One standard under the law

Public authority, financial resources, or political connections should not place anyone beyond fair scrutiny or due process.

02 / SMARTER OVERSIGHT

Identify concerns earlier

Help existing oversight institutions spot missing records, inconsistent data, and potential compliance issues while preserving human judgment.

03 / OPEN INNOVATION

Make room for better ideas

Encourage practical problem-solving through open challenges, independent evaluation, and meaningful citizen participation.

Featured proof of concept • AIC-001

From principles to a working demonstration

AIC-001 explores how automated checks can support government contract oversight. It combines a reproducible review of public award data with a separate, simulated pre-award screening demonstration.

Synthetic screening demonstration

Check before an award

The prototype tests whether specified document metadata is present, timely, and internally consistent. It shows how human reviewers could be alerted to questions before approval.

Important: The demonstration uses invented examples and deterministic rules—not a deployed AI model or actual government procurement files.

It does not authenticate signatures, interpret the FAR, establish legal noncompliance, or replace an authorized contracting officer.

New prototype module • AIC-001 v2.3

Business identity & address screening

Explore how consistent address formatting can reveal relationships across award recipients—and why shared addresses must be interpreted cautiously.

What we check

Shared addresses, registered-agent offices, PO boxes where a physical location is required, incomplete addresses, and conflicting addresses tied to the same business identifier.

Important limitations

All businesses, addresses, identifiers and awards in this module are invented. No live USPS, property, corporate registry, or geocoding verification is performed. An alert is not a fraud finding.

Try the address screening demonstration

Choose a test pattern to see the supporting records and a human-review recommendation.

The demonstration runs locally in your browser. It does not collect or transmit information.

Where this goes next

Build trust before seeking scale

Publish the evidence

Release clear methodology, source links, limitations, reproducible results, and correction procedures.

Invite independent review

Seek feedback from procurement professionals, technical researchers, and oversight practitioners; measure false alerts and missed issues.

Pursue an authorized pilot

Work with an existing institution to evaluate the approach on permitted, representative records with appropriate security and human oversight.

Future concepts include public innovation challenges and a citizen ideas forum. These are proposed programs, not currently operating services.
